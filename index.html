Before trying this in a real car

Safety comes first

Use the app while parked. Learn in a suitable place with qualified supervision.

Before moving

  • Adjust your seat and mirrors and fasten your seat belt while parked.
  • Check mirrors and blind spots for people, cyclists and vehicles.
  • Signal when appropriate; move only when the path is clear.

While parking

  • Move slowly and make small steering adjustments.
  • Keep checking around you; mirrors and cameras do not show everything.
  • If unsure, stop safely, check again, and ask your instructor.

Important

Do not use or look at this app while driving. Practice only in a legal, appropriate area with a qualified instructor. Follow local rules and your vehicle’s manual.

ParkSmart is an educational aid, not a substitute for professional instruction, local driving laws, or your vehicle’s manual.
